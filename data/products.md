# Nimbus Coffee Roasters — Product Notes

## The Meridian blend

Meridian is our flagship espresso blend: 60 percent Brazilian Cerrado, 30 percent
Colombian Huila, 10 percent Ethiopian Yirgacheffe. Roasted medium-dark to a first
crack plus ninety seconds.

Tasting notes are dark chocolate, toasted hazelnut and a fig sweetness on the finish.
It is forgiving under pressure variation, which is why we recommend it for high-volume
service.

Meridian rests for a minimum of seven days after roasting before we ship it. Peak
flavour is between days 10 and 28.

## The Halcyon single origin

Halcyon is a washed Kenyan AA from Nyeri County, grown between 1,700 and 1,900 metres.
We roast it light, dropping the batch shortly after first crack.

Expect blackcurrant, tomato leaf and a pronounced grapefruit acidity. It is
deliberately bright and divides opinion; we do not recommend it as a default filter
option for cafes serving a general audience.

Halcyon is seasonal and typically available from March to September.

## Decaf

Our decaf is the Meridian blend processed by the Swiss Water method, which uses no
chemical solvents. Caffeine removal is 99.9 percent.

Decaf beans are roasted two degrees cooler than their caffeinated equivalent because
the decaffeination process makes them more heat-sensitive.

## Grind guidance

For espresso, start at 18 grams in and aim for 36 grams out in 28 to 32 seconds.
Adjust grind size before adjusting dose.

For filter, use a 1:16 ratio by weight, water at 94 degrees Celsius, and a total brew
time of three to four minutes.

Cold brew uses a 1:8 ratio, coarse grind, steeped 16 hours at room temperature, then
diluted to taste.

## Packaging and shelf life

All retail bags are 250 grams with a one-way degassing valve. Unopened, they keep for
twelve months from the roast date printed on the base.

Once opened, use within three weeks for best results. Store in a cool dark cupboard,
never in the refrigerator, where condensation damages the beans.

Wholesale customers receive 1 kilogram bags with the same valve and shelf life.
