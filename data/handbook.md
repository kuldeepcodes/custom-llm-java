# Nimbus Coffee Roasters — Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Our standard week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, during
which everyone is expected to be reachable. Outside core hours you may arrange your
schedule as suits you, provided your team knows when to expect you.

Roastery floor staff work fixed shifts because the equipment requires supervision.
Shift patterns are published four weeks in advance.

## Remote work

Office-based staff may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements are possible but must be agreed with your manager and reviewed every
six months.

We reimburse up to 400 pounds towards a home desk and chair, once every three years.
Submit receipts through the Expenses portal within 60 days of purchase.

## Time off

Everyone receives 27 days of paid annual leave, plus public holidays. Leave accrues
monthly and unused days may be carried over up to a maximum of five days.

Sick leave is not counted against annual leave. Notify your manager before 09:30 on
the first day of absence. Absences longer than seven consecutive days require a
doctor's note.

Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary carer and 8 weeks at full pay
for the secondary carer, available to all employees regardless of gender.

## Expenses

Travel booked more than 21 days in advance may be expensed without prior approval up
to 600 pounds. Anything above that, or booked later, needs written sign-off from a
department head.

Client entertainment is capped at 45 pounds per head. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days. Claims older than 90 days will not
be paid except in documented exceptional circumstances.

## Equipment

Laptops are replaced on a three-year cycle. If your machine fails before then, the IT
desk will repair or replace it at no cost to your budget.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours so the device can be
remotely wiped.
